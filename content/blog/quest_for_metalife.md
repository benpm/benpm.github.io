# Quest... for Metalife?

If you've read my [previous article](../projects/cellarium.md) articles, you'll know that it's hard to make cellular automata fast enough to compute interesting things. We are also limited by the finite space we inhabit.

What if...
  - We could compute on an infinite space?
  - We could accelerate computation times by an insane amount?
  - Distinct rulesets could exist in the same space, and interact with each other?

Hashlife is an algorithm that succeeds in computing cellular automata (CA) macro states.
