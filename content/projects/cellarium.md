# Cellarium
[**> Play Now**](https://benpm.github.io/cellarium")

Cellarium is a WebGL-based cellular automata simulator that allows users to create and explore complex patterns and behaviors. With dynamic rulesets and an infinite grid, Cellarium offers a unique platform for experimenting with cellular automata in real-time.

For a detailed breakdown of the implementation, see my [this post](../blog/cellular_automata_1.md).
